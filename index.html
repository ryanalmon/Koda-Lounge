Kodawari

Our Philosophy

Welcome to
Koda Lounge

In Japanese, Kodawari embodies the uncompromising and relentless pursuit of perfection, guiding everything we do. We don't settle for ordinary, mediocre, or mundane.

Instead, we seek the extraordinary, the exceptional, the sublime. We believe that every drink we craft is a catalyst for connection, inspiration, passion, and transformation.

We strive to create opportunities for you to learn something new, to share something meaningful, and to dream something big. Join us in our quest for excellence and discover your own Kodawari.

The Atmosphere

An Intimate
Sanctuary

Koda Lounge is a private haven where conversation flows as freely as the cocktails. Inspired by the great bars of Tokyo, London, and New York, we have created a space where excellence is not an aspiration — it is the only standard.

Each evening is a carefully curated experience. From the ice we sculpt by hand to the spirits we source from around the world, every detail is considered, every gesture intentional.

The Menu

Signature
Cocktails

I Our Signature

The Kodawari Martini

Japanese whisky, dry vermouth, a whisper of yuzu, expressed over a strip of lemon peel and served on Hinoki cedar wood

II Seasonal

Sakura Negroni

Roku gin, Campari, sweet vermouth, cherry blossom tincture, served over a hand-carved sphere with a burnt orange expression

III A San Francisco Classic

Fog City Old Fashioned

Bourbon, house-made miso caramel syrup, Angostura bitters, smoked tableside with Pacific cedar to honour our city by the bay

IV Chef's Choice

The Wabi-Sabi Sour

Umeshu, pisco, fresh lemon, egg white, finished with a mist of toasted sesame oil — beauty found in imperfection

V Bespoke

Omotenashi

A cocktail crafted entirely to your spirit and mood. Tell us how you feel, and we will tell your story in a glass

VI Light & Refreshing

Pacific Highball

Single malt Scotch, premium tonic, fresh wasabi-lime zest, long-poured over a spear of crystal ice — the perfection of simplicity
